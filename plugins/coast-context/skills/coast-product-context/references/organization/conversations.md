# Conversations and messages

Coast connects team communication to the work it concerns. A **thread** is the conversation context; a **message** is an item within it. The same message concepts appear in more than one context, but the context determines where a person returns when they open the conversation.

## Conversation contexts

- **Workspace discussion** keeps general team messages with a [workspace](workspaces.md).
- **Record discussion** keeps comments and updates with one standalone [record](../workflows/records.md), such as a work order. A comment found in search retains that record context.
- **Direct conversation** supports discussion with people outside a particular workspace or record thread. It is a communication context, not another record kind.

An [AI conversation](../ai-assistance.md) has a different scope, history, and run model. It should not be treated as a human workspace or record message thread.

## Message content and interactions

People can send authored text and attachments, reply within a conversation, mention people or groups, react to messages, and mark a message as urgent where the experience supports it. An authored message may later be edited or deleted under the applicable product rules. A mention is part of message content and can direct attention; it does not itself add the mentioned person to a workspace or prove that a notification was delivered. [People and access](people.md) own identity and membership.

Threads can also contain system entries associated with events such as record creation or updates. A system entry in a thread is distinct from the broader [activity feed](../across-workspaces/attention-and-activity.md#activity-and-mentions) and from a delivered notification. When explaining what happened, identify whether the evidence is a human message, system entry, record history event, or notification.

## Context and attention

A message's originating workspace or record remains important when it appears elsewhere, including [global search](../across-workspaces/search-and-navigation.md#search-scopes). Read positions, unread indicators, seen-by information, notification preferences, snoozing, and the mentions feed concern the person's relationship to messages. [Attention and activity](../across-workspaces/attention-and-activity.md) owns those states; the conversation owns the message and its context.
